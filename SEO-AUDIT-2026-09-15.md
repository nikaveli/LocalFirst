# LocalFirst SEO audit and release plan

September 15, 2026. Public site: https://localfirstonline.com. Compared live production with the unpublished $349 messaging update. This audit covers technical SEO, local content, contact paths, and sampled browser accessibility/performance. It is not a guarantee of ranking, leads, or full accessibility conformance.

## Assessment

The crawlability foundation is sound. The highest-value gaps are a dedicated service page, clear package details, reliable lead capture and measurement, verified local proof, and mobile performance evidence. The current public site's $497 offer is a pending publication difference, not a newly discovered technical defect.

### Confirmed baseline

- Four live routes return 200 with server-rendered content, distinct titles/descriptions, self-referencing canonicals, social metadata, and parseable Organization/WebSite/Service/page JSON-LD.
- Live regression check passed 222 checks: 57 internal links/fragments, 54 assets, sitemap, robots, missing-page behavior, and www redirect.
- Independently checked HTTP and www URLs return 308 to HTTPS apex, retaining path/query.
- Google Search is permitted by robots.txt. Cloudflare's training crawler restrictions are distinct from Googlebot restrictions and were preserved.
- Sampled desktop/mobile pages had no horizontal overflow or JavaScript runtime errors.
- Home and About had confirmed small-text contrast gaps. Reduced-motion home also lacked keyboard access to the horizontal method region.
- Contact form opens an email draft; it does not deliver a message from the website. No marketing analytics or conversion events were identified in the reviewed application source or rendered scripts. This does not rule out hosting-level analytics or DNS-verified Search Console.
- The site displays Google reviews and BBB credentials but has no direct verification links. Reviews/ages/counts are static and were not independently reverified.

## Remaining work, in priority order

1. **Verify Search Console and submit sitemap.** Inspect all five canonical URLs after release, check chosen canonicals, indexation, manual actions, and real queries. Account data was unavailable; a search-engine listing is not evidence of complete Google indexing.
2. **Set up reliable form delivery and lead measurement.** Select/confirm email backend and credentials, delivery inbox, error/confirmation behavior, and minimal data collection. Track call clicks, SMS clicks, and successful form submissions distinctly. A click is not a delivered message or booked visit. Establish weekly qualified-inquiry and booked-visit totals, not just traffic.
3. **Confirm and link the official Google and BBB profiles.** Add only verified URLs to visible proof links and appropriate Organization sameAs fields. Keep business name/phone/service area consistent. Confirm review count and dates; do not invent review schema or credentials.
4. **Add real case studies.** Start with a restaurant and a med spa: actual work, location, specific deliverables, and owner-approved photos/results. Existing visit videos are evidence of visits, not automatically paid-client work or measured business outcomes.
5. **Confirm booking details.** Photo/video quantities, visit duration, delivery timeline, usage rights, profile access, and any travel restrictions should be answered when Nicholas supplies them. Do not invent guarantees.
6. **Measure field performance.** PageSpeed API returned HTTP 429, so no fresh Lighthouse or Chrome UX Report score is claimed. Initial unthrottled browser observations varied (home desktop LCP around 9.1 s, mobile 2.1 s; CLS 0). These are single opening observations, not 75th-percentile field metrics, and omit in-flight transfers. Prior audit scores from September 10 predate later video quality changes. Preserve the approved video; investigate non-video requests and loading independently if field data shows a problem.
7. **Local reputation and links.** Complete GBP information and pursue relevant genuine client/community references. Website changes alone cannot supply local prominence or change searcher distance.
8. **Publish useful supporting content as evidence becomes available.** No need for a generic daily blog or near-duplicate city pages. One useful service page plus real work is the first step.

## What is not required

Google does not require special AI schema or an llms.txt file for AI Overview/AI Mode eligibility. Conventional crawlability, useful text, accurate business details, and evidence remain relevant. See [Google AI search guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features).

Visible FAQs help buyers, but adding FAQ markup is not a current rich-result opportunity: Google discontinued FAQ rich results in May 2026. See [Google documentation updates](https://developers.google.com/search/updates). Likewise, self-serving business reviews are not a way to obtain organic review stars; see [Google review guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/blog/2019/09/making-review-rich-results-more-helpful).

An exact 60-character title, 160-character description, one H1, or minimum word count is not a Google ranking requirement. The project's title/description lengths are editorial checks. Relevant, useful content matters more than passing a cosmetic score. See [Google SEO Starter Guide](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/seo-starter-guide).

## Release verification

Final build, browser checks, and public deployment evidence will be recorded with the release. No unsupported promise that the site now meets everything needed for success is made: measurement, real customer proof, local reputation, and qualified inquiries remain ongoing work.

Release preparation: Next.js production build and ESLint pass; 19 redirect/media unit tests pass. Updated preview passes 284 SEO checks. Three viewport widths (390, 1024, 1440) were sampled for the service/contact/portfolio pages; FAQ interaction works and no overflow was detected. Scroll configuration attributes, video elements, media sources and choreography code were compared with the prior commit and are unchanged. The slow-frame browser stress test did not meet its minimum forward-frame threshold, including an isolated run (restaurant 9, opening 12, med spa 16 forward frames; all scenes reached the end and rewound). This remains a performance warning, not a pass. Video code/assets/configuration are unchanged; do not attribute the failure to the copy update or claim it is resolved. Normal playback verification is recorded separately.

Normal mobile browser playback passed in an isolated run: opening film 33 forward/219 reverse frames, restaurant 30/123, med spa 87/102; all rewound near the beginning, cache limits held, and no JavaScript errors or mobile MP4 requests occurred. Slow-network performance remains the separate warning above.

Final targeted accessibility recheck: no color-contrast or scrollable-region-focusable violations in the corrected About credentials, home method region, or new service page. New service page screenshots were reviewed at desktop and mobile widths. Final Next.js build passes, including the preserved Contact introduction styling.
