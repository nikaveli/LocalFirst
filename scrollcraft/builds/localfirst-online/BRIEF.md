## Page grammar

**Filmic one-shot.** The page has one linear sales argument and the supplied full-bleed films must lead it. The acts hand off by overlap and physical slide, even though their worlds are intentionally distinct.

The other grammars lost for specific reasons:

- Chaptered editorial forbids the full-bleed scrub hero.
- Live surface is for an operable software product, not an on-site service.
- Continuous world contradicts the requested distinct scenes.
- Typographic poster forbids the photographic ground that is the product proof.
- Gallery or catalog forbids persuasive labels and a single hero claim, but this page must convert.
- Split stage requires a two-sided comparison to remain present for the whole page.
- Rhythmic cutlist bans the long, controlled scrub and dwell the supplied films need.

## Signature move

**The Developing Stack.** Restaurant and med-spa films scrub inside full-screen photographic sheets. Each finished sheet rises over the previous world and leaves a slim exposure tab behind. At the peak, the separate tabs align into one proof line: "Shot here. Built to be chosen." The movement is custom page code driven from the proof act's scroll progress, not a Scrollcraft engine device.

The LocalFirst-to-restaurant handoff uses the same developing-sheet motion as the restaurant-to-med-spa handoff. The outgoing LocalFirst stage stays visually locked while its final Photos, Video, 360°, and Reviews frames scrub. The restaurant film becomes a viewport-fixed overlay only during the final 0.52 viewport before the proof act begins, sliding upward as it fades from transparent to opaque. The reveal completes exactly as the restaurant scrub starts, eliminating both the early arrival and the static hold while keeping the document boundary hidden. On mobile, the hero begins as a centered film panel that keeps the Google profile phone fully visible, then expands into the full viewport as the copy clears.
